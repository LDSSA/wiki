## Curriculum overview

The detailed curriculum is maintained in the [curriculum-development repository](https://github.com/LDSSA/curriculum-development/tree/master/curriculum).

The Starters Academy consists of a Bootcamp, six Specializations, six Hackathons and one Capstone project.

## Bootcamp and Specialization 1

The Bootcamp opens Specialization 1 with two days of remote classes. Specialization 1 contains **17 mandatory Small Learning Units (SLU01–17)** and four optional units: **SLU18, SLU19, SLU32 and SLU64**. It ends with Hackathon 1.

## Specializations 2–6

Each remaining Specialization contains three Big Learning Units (BLUs) focused on a data science topic and ends with a Hackathon.

## Learning Units

Learning Units contain teaching material and an exercise notebook graded by the Portal. SLUs are shorter units used in Specialization 1; BLUs are longer units used in Specializations 2–6.

A score of at least **16/20** is required to pass a Learning Unit. You may submit multiple attempts before its deadline. Only deadline-valid submissions count for progression and certificate eligibility. Late work remains available for learning but normally does not restore eligibility. If a documented Portal incident prevented an on-time submission, staff may validate the recorded late submission as an exception.

## Hackathons

Hackathons are day-long team exercises in which students solve and present a data science problem. Hackathon 1 is required to continue after Specialization 1. See the [course overview](https://ldssa.github.io/wiki/Starters%20Academy%20%28LDSSA%29/01-Starters-Academy-%28Course%29/#2-progression-and-graduation-certificate) for the full progression and certification rules.

## Capstone

The Capstone is the individual final project. Each student completes a data science project and submits the required reports, application and code. See [Capstone Project](https://ldssa.github.io/wiki/Starters%20Academy%20%28LDSSA%29/05-Capstone-Project/).
